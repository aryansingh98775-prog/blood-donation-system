Type O Negative: The Universal Red Cell Donor

O Negative blood has neither A nor B antigens, and is Rh negative. It can be safely transfused to patients of ANY blood type in sudden emergency rooms and ambulances when there is zero time to cross-match.

Type AB Positive: The Universal Recipient

AB Positive patients have both A and B antigens and Rh protein. Their immune system recognizes all foreign blood cells, allowing them to safely receive red blood cells from ALL 8 blood types.
