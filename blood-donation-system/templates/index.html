Simple 4-Step Process

How Blood Donation Works

The entire whole blood donation process takes less than an hour, with the actual blood drawing taking only 8 to 10 minutes.

01

Sign Up & Profile

Register as a voluntary donor with your blood group, contact phone, and city to join our instant emergency responder registry.

02

Book Appointment

Select your nearest authorized blood center or mobile blood drive camp at a date and time convenient for you.

03

Quick Donation

Trained phlebotomists perform a quick health check (pulse, hemoglobin, blood pressure) and safely collect 1 unit (approx 450ml).

04

Save 3 Lives

Your single unit is processed into red blood cells, platelets, and plasma, directly giving second chances to trauma & surgery patients.
